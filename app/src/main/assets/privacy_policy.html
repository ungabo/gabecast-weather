GabeCast Privacy Policy

GabeCast is a free, ad-free U.S. weather app. The MVP does not require an account and does not run a custom backend server.

Location data is used only to request weather information from the National Weather Service and to save locations on this device. Manual search is supported if you do not want to grant location permission.

The app asks you to enter a contact email before it requests weather data. That email is saved only on this device and sent to NOAA/NWS as part of the request User-Agent header because public weather services ask client apps to identify a contact.

Radar imagery is requested from NOAA/NWS public radar image services when you open the Radar screen or refresh radar.

ZIP code lookup data is derived from GeoNames postal code data, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

Weather data may be delayed or unavailable. Follow official emergency instructions during severe weather.
